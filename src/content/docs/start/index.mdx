In this tutorial we install OpenMausBot on a Mac, get a bot to answer, then give it a real job: **tomorrow at 9 a.m., summarize the top 5 Hacker News stories and send them to you.**

You need a Mac and a Claude (Pro or Max) or ChatGPT subscription. It is quickest if Claude Code or Codex is already signed in on this Mac.

<Steps>

1. **Install the app**

   Download the `.dmg` for your chip from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot/releases/latest) and drag it into Applications. Or let your AI agent do it:

   ```text title="Prompt"
   Download the .dmg for this Mac's chip from the latest milind-soni/OpenMausBot GitHub release,
   verify it is signed and notarized with spctl --assess, install it to /Applications and open it.
   ```

   You'll see a chat app with one contact on the left: **MausBot**.

2. **Connect your AI**

   On first launch it asks you to **Connect an AI** (this site calls these "engines"). Pick the Claude or Codex you already use: if it is signed in on this Mac it is reused. If not, you sign in on their own website. No new account needed.

3. **Say hello**

   Click **MausBot** and send:

   ```text
   In one sentence: which model are you, and what can you do for me?
   ```

   Once it answers, the whole chain works: app → your local `claude` / `codex` CLI → model → back. OpenMausBot uses the CLI already signed in on your Mac, with no other service in between.

4. **Give it a real job**

   ```text
   Every day at 9:00, open news.ycombinator.com, summarize the top 5 stories in one sentence each
   with links, and send them to me. Run it once now so I can see it.
   ```

   It usually runs the job right away and sends you the five summaries, then creates a daily 9:00 routine for itself. A card appears in the chat with the schedule and the next few run times.

5. **Check the routine exists**

   - **Simple interface** (the default on a new install): click **MausBot** at the top of the chat. Under **Routines** in the profile on the right you see the routine and its next run time.
   - **Advanced interface**: click **Automations** under **Tools** in the sidebar. Open the routine to see its next run time, or click **Run now** to run it once.

</Steps>

You now have a bot that works for you every day. Your first briefing arrives tomorrow at 9.

:::caution[On a Mac, bots have your permissions]
New bots start at **Just do it**: they run commands without asking, as your macOS user, and can read your personal files. To have a bot ask first, click the bot's name at the top of the chat, then the gear icon, and in **Bot settings** pick **Ask me first** under **Before \{bot\} acts**; [approval levels](/concepts/approval-levels/) explains the four. For long-term use, move bots to [a separate server](/guides/home-server/).
:::

:::note[Routines don't run while the Mac sleeps]
The app on your computer runs the schedule, so a sleeping computer or a quit app runs nothing, and the app can't wake a sleeping Mac. While plugged in, it keeps the Mac from idle-sleeping for the hour before a routine is due, but a closed lid still sleeps. For 24/7 reliability see [where your bots should run](/concepts/where-to-run/).
:::
