## Three habits for writing a good brief

1. Demo first, real data second. Have the bot build the result on fake data, settle the format, then connect real accounts.
2. Write acceptance criteria into the request. "Every item needs a link", "tell me when it fails", "stay quiet if nothing changed" work better than correcting it afterwards.
3. Save what worked as a skill. Say "save how we did this as a skill" and the next similar job follows the same path.
